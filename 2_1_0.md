# MeshGod4000 2.1.0
## Texture Painter, Decimator and a new layout

This is a bigger update. I've added painting directly on models, two new Mesh Operations and side tabs inspired by Blender.

## 🟢 Added

### Texture Painter

You can now paint Albedo / Base Color directly on your model in Unity. It works with static and skinned meshes.

Select the faces you want to work on in Modeler, then switch to Texture Painter. Want to paint the whole model? Select all faces first.

There are five tools:

- **Brush:** paint directly on the model.
- **Eraser:** remove your paint and bring back the original colors of the working copy.
- **Bucket:** fill a connected part of the model, within your selection and enabled materials.
- **Recolor:** replace one color with another. Adjust the tolerance and optionally keep the existing light and dark detail.
- **Stamp:** place an image onto visible selected faces. You can resize and rotate it, and transparent parts of the image won't paint.

You can use custom brushes and hold **Alt + mouse wheel** to change their size. Models with several materials are supported too. Pick All, None or individual material slots from the dropdown.

Switching tabs or moving to another model keeps your working draft. Undo / Redo is supported while editing, and you save the result as a copy without overwriting the original assets.

For now, this is color painting. It doesn't paint normal maps, roughness or other material channels.

### Small UVs and palette-colored models

If your low-poly models use tiny UV islands or a single palette color per triangle, you can use the **Face Color copy** option to color whole triangles.

MeshGod detects these layouts and shows the relevant information. Tools that don't fit this mode, such as Stamp, are disabled with a tooltip explaining why.

Normal texture copies keep the source dimensions, including rectangular textures. Face Color copies may need more texture space to store the individual triangle colors.

With ordinary texture painting, faces sharing the same UV pixels still share the painted color.

### Decimator (Beta)

A new operation in the **Mesh** category. Click it and the button expands to show a **Remaining %** slider. No extra window.

- **100%** gives you the original mesh. **0%** removes all faces.
- During longer calculations, the model preview updates about once per second.
- A progress bar shows how the calculation is going.
- Previous calculations are reused where possible, making repeated slider adjustments faster.
- Separate pieces inside one mesh are processed too.
- Turn the operation off or switch to another one to apply the requested result.
- Press **Escape** to cancel. Undo / Redo works after applying.

Use it to make lower-detail versions and judge the result by eye. Stronger reduction can change the shape, shading and texture appearance. Some geometry cannot reach the exact requested percentage without breaking the algorithm's safety checks.

### Limited Dissolve (Beta)

Also in **Mesh**. Select some faces and click it to reduce their triangle count while keeping changes to the shape limited.

It only works on your selection. Unselected faces stay untouched, and shared selection boundaries are protected. If everything is selected, it uses the whole-mesh fast path.

There is no slider here. One click, with Undo / Redo support. Texture appearance and shading can still change, so check the result on your model.

Both operations currently work on **static meshes** and require unpacked prefabs. They keep the original vertex records, so reducing triangles doesn't necessarily reduce the vertex buffer size.

### Side tabs inspired by Blender

MeshGod now has four tabs down the left side:

- **Modeler:** mesh operations and face selection.
- **Texture Painter:** painting tools.
- **Settings:** preferences.
- **Community:** Discord, documentation, tutorials and feedback.

Selection controls stay in Modeler. Texture Painter has its own space without the mesh-editing controls mixed in.

The Community trophy is still there, with the same usage-based color progression as before.

### Beta labels

New experimental operations can now show **Beta** on the right side of their buttons. Decimator and Limited Dissolve have this label in 2.1.0.

These are new tools and may still have issues. If something doesn't look right, contact me, **Adam, on Discord** through the Community tab. A screenshot and a description of what you were doing will help.

## 🟡 Changed

- Texture Painter is accessed through its own tab, not the Modeler operation list.
- Painting tools have an icon toolbar with tooltips.
- Temporary Texture Paint and Decimator entries have been removed from Unity's top menu.
- The MeshGod Operations guide now includes Decimator and Limited Dissolve.
