## [1.4.0] – 2025-10-12  

### Combo Operations

**Summary:**  
Introduced *Combo* operations, added *Remove Unused Materials* operation, enabled favoriting of user-chosen operations in the Mesh God Inspector, and enhanced overall workflow and usability.

---

### 🟢 Added

- **Combo** – Execute multiple mesh operations in sequence automatically.  
  - Users can create their own combos by duplicating default combos in `MeshGod3000 → Resources → MeshOperations`.  
  - Only **instant operations** are executed in a combo; permanent tools are skipped.  
  - Newly created combos immediately appear in the Inspector for easy access.

- **Remove Unused Materials** – Automatically detects and removes materials that are not assigned to any triangles in a mesh, cleaning up your assets.

- **Settings TAB** – Customize your Mesh God 3000 workflow:  
  - Hide the main “big button” if desired.  
  - Use only the compact “small” button for minimal UI.  
  - Change the **Ghost Material** color to adjust preview mesh appearance in the Scene view.  
  - *(Future updates will add more workflow customization options here.)*

- **Favorite Star** – Mark your preferred operations with a star.  
  - Favorited operations are pinned at the top of the Inspector, boosting workflow speed and convenience.

---

### 🟡 Changed

- **Bug Fixes & Stability** – Minor bug fixes and improved asset stability and workflow ergonomics.  
- **Enhanced Tooltips** – All mesh operations now provide clearer guidance:  
  - Prefab unpacking requirements are highlighted when needed.  
  - Tooltips now help users understand necessary conditions for each operation.
